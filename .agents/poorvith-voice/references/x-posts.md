# X (Twitter) — Posts & Replies

## Original posts
Style is a mix — there's no single fixed template. Before drafting, figure out (or ask if unclear) what kind of post this is, since the right voice depends on it:
- Short/punchy opinion take
- Educational/informative (can be a thread)
- Building-in-public update (progress, a learning, a milestone)

Ask him which of these (or something else) this post is, if it's not obvious from what he's told you, rather than defaulting to one style.

General rules regardless of type:
- Fact-check any claim before posting (see core rules in main SKILL.md).
- Draft the full post — don't give him a fill-in-the-blank template.
- Keep it tight. X rewards density; cut filler ruthlessly.
- Voice and emoji usage follow `references/avoid-ai-tells.md` — casual/build-in-public takes can carry 0–1 emoji if it fits, technical/informative posts should stay emoji-free.

## Replies to other people's posts
His posture on replies is **not fixed** — it genuinely depends on the post and what he wants to accomplish. Before drafting a reply, always ask him:
1. What's the post he's replying to (paste it or link it), and
2. What's his angle — agreeing and adding value, pushing back/adding nuance, asking a genuine question, or something else.

Do not guess his angle and draft a reply speculatively. Get the angle first, then write it.
