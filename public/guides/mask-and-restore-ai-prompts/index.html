Mask and restore AI prompts

Mask detected values, copy only the masked prompt, keep the session key private, then restore a fabricated response locally.

Open the mask-and-restore demo

CLI equivalent

npx aiscrubber mask prompt.txt --key session.json --output prompt.masked.txt
npx aiscrubber unmask reply.txt --key session.json --output reply.restored.txt

Limitations

Do not send the session key to an external model. Altered placeholder names cannot be restored automatically.
