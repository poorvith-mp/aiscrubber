Scrub logs before sharing

Paste a synthetic incident log, review each replacement, then copy or download the reviewed text.

Example

Host: 192.168.1.44
Contact: ops@example.com

Open the incident-log demo

CLI equivalent

npx aiscrubber scrub incident.log --output incident.clean.log

Limitations

Detection is pattern-based. Review the result. Names and street addresses may need a custom word rule or manual selection.
